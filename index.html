THE PROBLEM

Problem in the field.

Yet every farm already generates valuable information — soil readings, crop images, GPS data, and payment trails — but it remains unused.

Transition: AgriGuard AI turns farm data into trusted proof, enabling insurance, credit, input access, and market opportunities.

Farming is risky — and farmers carry the burden
Pests and diseases destroy harvests
Climate shocks hit crops without warning
Farmers sell at poor prices due to weak market information
Banks refuse loans and insurance due to lack of verified data

Founder & CEO
Gloria Mbatha is a Strathmore University graduate with over six years of experience working directly with smallholder farmers across Africa. She saw the injustice of productive farmers being denied credit simply because they lacked records. Inspired by how vehicles and tractors are financed through monitoring tools, she asked: “Why can’t farms be financed using their own data?” Gloria founded AgriGuard AI to make that possible — combining low-cost farm sensors, GIS intelligence, and farmer-approved mobile-money insights to create trustworthy digital profiles that financiers can rely on.
